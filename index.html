Employee Hub Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This policy explains how Adventure Subaru, Inc. handles information in the Adventure Subaru Employee Hub app. The app is an employee resource for announcements, events, attendance, ticket requests, and communication with event organizers. Access requires an account provided by Adventure Subaru.

Information the app collects

  • Account information: Employee names, email addresses, account identifiers, account roles, and access status. Sign-in credentials are processed by our authentication provider, Supabase.
  • Event activity: Attendance and volunteer sign-ups, ticket quantities and request status, event invitations, and related timestamps.
  • Messages and content: Messages to organizers, organizer replies, event group updates, message read status, and event and announcement text.
  • Attachments: Photos and other images that administrators choose to upload to events or announcements.
  • Reports and message blocks: Content reports, reasons and optional details, copies of reported content, reviewer decisions and notes, and the accounts between which direct messages are blocked.
  • Notification information: Push notification tokens associated with employee accounts, notification preferences, and notification scheduling and delivery records.
  • Technical information: Authentication and service logs, which may include IP addresses, user-agent information, timestamps, sign-in events, and errors used for security and troubleshooting.

This information can be associated with an employee's account. The app does not request the employee's device location or phone address book. An event's location is information about the event, not a measurement of the employee's location.

How information is used

We use information to authenticate employees, manage access, display events and announcements, maintain attendance rosters, process ticket requests, exchange messages, deliver notifications and reminders, review reported content, enforce direct-message blocks, and operate and secure the app. Event responses and message read status support these features.

We do not sell Employee Hub information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking or targeted advertising.

Who can access information

Event organizers can see relevant sign-ups, ticket requests, and direct messages for their events. Within the app, a direct conversation is available to the employee who sent the message and the event organizer. Group updates are shared with employees who have access to the event. Invitation-only events are restricted to invited employees and the event creator.

Reported content is also available to the designated content reviewer, Tyler Chadwick. This can include a copy of a reported direct message or organizer reply, report details, and reported event or announcement attachments, including attachments from invitation-only events. General administrator or user-management access does not automatically grant report-review access. Personnel with authorized backend access and our service providers may access information as needed to maintain and secure the service.

Some event and announcement attachments are delivered through public file links. Anyone who obtains one of those links may be able to view the attachment. Invitation-only event attachments use restricted access links.

We use service providers to operate the app:

  • Supabase: Account authentication, database services, file storage, and related technical logs.
  • Expo: Push notification delivery, including processing notification content and storing push tokens.
  • Apple and Google: Platform notification services used to deliver notifications to iOS and Android devices.

Information is provided to these services as needed to operate the app. Their services may process information on servers outside your state or country. We may also disclose information when required by law or as necessary to protect the security and rights of employees and Adventure Subaru.

Notifications and device permissions

You can change available notification preferences in the app and disable notifications in your device settings. Disabling push notifications does not remove your account or event records.

Administrators can choose images for attachments through the device's image selection features. You can manage available photo permissions in your device settings. Notification previews may display message or event content on your lock screen, depending on your device settings.

Reporting content and blocking direct messages

You can report content using the app's Report Content controls. A report records your account, the content involved, your selected reason, any details you provide, and a copy of relevant content for review. You can view your own reports. Tyler Chadwick is the designated reviewer and can review reports, record a decision, and remove or hide content where appropriate. Contact Tyler using the email below if you need help reporting content.

You can block direct messages through Message Options and manage your own blocks in the app. Blocking prevents new direct messages and organizer replies between the two accounts, including the related push notifications. Existing conversation history remains available. Blocking does not hide company events, announcements, or event group updates, and does not stop event reminders or ticket approval and decline notifications.

Retention and deletion

Account information, event records, messages, attachments, and notification records are stored in the service. Signing out, uninstalling the app, or deactivating an account does not automatically delete those records.

You can delete your account in the app by opening More, selecting Delete My Account, typing DELETE, and confirming the deletion. This removes your sign-in account and profile, your event responses and the conversations stored with those responses, your authored event group updates, invitations addressed to you, notification preferences, registered push tokens, message blocks, and notification records associated with your account. You will need an administrator to provide a new account if you want to use the app again.

Company events and announcements remain available after their creator deletes an account, with the creator's account link removed. Attachments used by those shared company records may also remain. Organizer replies stored in another employee's conversation are not automatically removed when the organizer deletes their account. Report records, including content copies, report details, and review notes, also remain after account deletion, although links to the deleted account are removed. Those retained records may still contain names or other personal information.

Retention depends on the information's purpose, operational needs, service-provider settings, and applicable recordkeeping requirements. Technical logs and backups may have separate retention periods.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information that remains after account deletion, or if you cannot use the in-app deletion feature, contact us using the email below. We will review your request, verify your identity as needed, and explain any information that must be retained and the reason for retaining it. Deleted information may remain in backups until those backups expire.

Security

We use account authentication and access controls to help protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Keep your sign-in credentials private and contact us if you believe your account has been accessed without authorization.

Children

The Employee Hub is intended for authorized employees and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, contact us.
